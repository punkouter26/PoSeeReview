GFX & Sound FX (G1–G10)

Captured from the running app (https://localhost:5001) in headless Chrome with GPU + WebGPU, using faked comic data so no paid generation ran. Items marked "harness" come from the standalone module test pages. 2026-10-21.

G1 + G2 · Generation wait

Before: a spinner-free stepper over empty frames, nothing else moving.

G2 + G3 · Comic arrives, score lands

The comic is printed (CMYK halftone plates sliding into register) while a WebGPU particle burst fires from the score ring; above 50 the backdrop ripples and a sub-bass thump lands.

G3 · Too normal to draw

Grey motes pop off the icon and flop to the floor; a sad-trombone cue replaces the generic "empty" blip.

G4 + G5 · Hall of Fame

Rows are damped springs: climbers overshoot, fallers bounce. A climb into #1 throws confetti made of tiny inked comic panels with a fanfare. Glass rows bend what is behind them (Chromium, full tier) and catch one pointer-following light.

G6 · Map

A strangeness heat glow in MapLibre’s own WebGL context (pulses at full tier); tapping a pin flies to it with a panned whoosh.

G7 · 3D mode

New "3D" toggle: the strip becomes a tilting diorama (parallax occlusion over cut-out depth). On WebGPU a Depth-Anything model refines it in a worker.

G8 · Duel

An arc strikes down the card, leaning toward the stranger comic; the winner’s half floods; both motifs answer each other. The card slams and shakes on the strike.

G10 · Share / save, and coming back

A printed copy curls off and flies into the button, then CERTIFIED STRANGE stamps the original. Back navigation now spreads in as an ink blot from the tap point.

G9 · Soundtrack

Audio only: a four-bar loop seeded by place id, voiced by score (scale, tempo, grit). Opt-in via the new Music chip in the account menu; ducks under narration.
